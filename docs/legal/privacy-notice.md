## A. For merchants and their staff

**Who we are.** Merchantflo ⟨legal entity name, CIN, registered address⟩ ("Merchantflo", "we")
provides payment-resilience software: scheduling UPI AutoPay debits, routing payments across a
merchant's own payment providers, and reporting.

**Our role.** For your customers' data we act as your **data processor** under the Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: we process it only on your instructions, under our Data
Processing Agreement. For your own account and your staff's dashboard accounts, we are the data
fiduciary and this notice applies directly.

**What we collect about you and your staff.** Business details you give at onboarding; names and
work e-mail addresses of dashboard users; sign-in records (time, IP address, success or failure);
actions taken in the dashboard (for your audit trail).

**Why.** To provide the service, secure accounts (including two-factor sign-in), keep an audit
trail you can rely on in disputes, bill you, and meet legal obligations.

**Where.** In India only: Amazon Web Services, Mumbai region, including backups.

**How long.** While your account is active; see the [retention policy](retention-policy.md).

**Your rights.** Access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal under the DPDP Act. Contact
⟨grievance officer name⟩ at privacy@merchantflo.in. We respond within ⟨30⟩ days.
